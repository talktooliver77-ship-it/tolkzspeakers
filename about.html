About Us

The bureau behind the voices.

Tolkz Speakers USA is a U.S.-registered speakers bureau committed to marketing and branding speakers globally — helping them secure paid speaking engagements nationally and internationally.

Who We Are

Built in the United States. Reaching the world.

Tolkz Speakers USA is a United States limited liability company, formed on March 31, 2026, with its address of record at 3387 Brownlow Ave, St Louis Park, MN 55426.

The bureau's platform connects aspiring and professional speakers with opportunities to showcase their voices, starting in the U.S. and Cameroon. Through curated events, coaching and exposure to international stages, Tolkz empowers speakers to build influence and grow their speaking careers.

Tolkz also works alongside the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS), the certification body founded by Tolkz's founder, so that the speakers it represents can back their talent with recognized professional credentials.

Our Vision

To be the best speakers bureau for African and African-American speakers — the bureau the world calls first when it wants those voices on its stages.

What We Stand For

Credibility before visibility. Tolkz documents what it claims, vets the associations it makes, and holds a simple standard: no claim beyond its proof.

Where We Work

Headquartered in Minnesota, USA, with speakers and partners across the United States, Cameroon, Africa and the wider diaspora.

The Founder

Oliver Nshom — Founder & CEO

Oliver Nshom has been a professional speaker for more than 17 years. After a 2023 breakthrough invitation to speak in Singapore, his stages have extended to the United States, Spain, India and beyond.

He is the founder of CANASA, the Cameroon National Speakers Association, and of the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS), which delivers the CES, CPS and CPTC professional speaker certifications. He founded Tolkz Speakers USA to build the bureau he believes African and African-American speakers deserve: one that markets them seriously, brands them professionally and puts them on paid stages around the world.

For speaking and coaching inquiries about Oliver directly, write to speaking@olivernshom.com. For bureau and corporate matters, write to ceo@tolkzspeakers.com.
