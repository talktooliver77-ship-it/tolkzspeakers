The TIPS Academy

Talent opens doors.
Certification keeps them open.

Tolkz Speakers USA connects its speakers and clients to professional certification through the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS) — the TIPS Academy. Three certifications, earned in order, assessed at every step: no certificate on experience alone.

Ask About Enrollment Visit the TIPS Campus

The Certification Pathway

CES → CPS → CPTC

Each level is independently enrollable and progressive: the next level unlocks as you pass the one before it. Candidates must score at least 70% on both the knowledge examination and the practical assessment at their level. Founding cohorts begin January 11, 2027 in the United States, Canada, Europe and Cameroon. United States tuition is shown below; tuition for other countries of residence is confirmed on application.

Beginner · Level 1

Certified Emerging Speaker (CES)

$495 USD · 4 weeks

  • For new and emerging speakers building their foundation
  • Speech craft, structure, delivery and stage confidence
  • Assessed speech + knowledge examination (70% / 70%)

Professional · Level 2

Certified Professional Speaker (CPS)

$1,495 USD · 8–12 weeks

  • For working speakers moving to professional, paid stages
  • Keynote development, positioning and the business of speaking
  • Entry: CES completion, or approved experience-based direct entry
  • Assessed professional keynote + knowledge examination (70% / 70%)

Advanced · Level 3

Certified Professional Trainer & Coach (CPTC)

$2,995 USD · 12–16 weeks

  • For experienced speakers who train and coach others
  • Training design, facilitation and coaching methodology
  • Entry: CPS completion, or approved advanced direct entry
  • Assessed training / coaching demonstration + knowledge examination (70% / 70%)
Enquire About CPTC

The Complete Certification Pathway — $3,995 USD

One enrollment covering all three certifications, unlocked in order as each level is passed. Bought separately in the United States, the three programs total $4,985 — the Complete Pathway saves $990. Enrollment is selection-first: candidates apply, are placed at the right level, and receive their enrollment details directly. There is no public checkout — contact the bureau to begin.

About the Texas Institute of Public Speaking

The Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS) owns and operates the Academy: it delivers the training, administers the examinations and assessments, keeps student records and issues every Academy certificate. Training is delivered through the TIPS Campus at tipscampus.com, with full program details at tipstexas.org.

Partnership Note

TIPS is in discussion with the Cameroon National Speakers Association (CANASA) regarding a proposed professional certification partnership. Any such arrangement will be announced if and when a formal agreement is concluded.
