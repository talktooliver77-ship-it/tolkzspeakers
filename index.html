What Tolkz Does

One bureau. Three ways to work with us.

Tolkz markets and brands speakers globally, helping them secure paid speaking engagements nationally and internationally — and gives organizers a trusted desk to book from.

1

Book a Speaker

Conferences, corporate events, churches, schools and summits: tell the bureau about your event and Tolkz will match it with the right voice from its roster — and handle the booking conversation end to end.

Browse the roster →

2

Get Represented

Speakers with a message and a mission can apply to join the Tolkz roster. Approved speakers are profiled to organizers and positioned for paid engagements in the United States and internationally.

Apply to join →

3

Train & Certify

Through the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS), Tolkz connects speakers to professional certification — CES, CPS and CPTC — so skill is not just claimed, it is credentialed.

Explore the TIPS Academy →

How Booking Works

From first inquiry to standing ovation

Confirm the booking

Fees, travel and terms are agreed in writing — clearly, before anyone commits.
